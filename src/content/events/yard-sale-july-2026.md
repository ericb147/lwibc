## About the Event

Please bring in items you'd like to donate for the youth to sell. All proceeds will go to the youth and items will be taken to a donation center afterwards so please do not donate items you want back.

Due to space limitations we will be limiting items to the following that we hope will sell easily:

1) Working electronics
2) Tools
3) Working Household items- small appliances, picture frames, blenders, pyrex dishes, etc.
4) Kids toys or books
5) Kids clothes
5) Craft items
6) Small furniture items

Please price your items before dropping them off. I do have price stickers and can leave those on the drop off table. Drop off will be in the Fellowship Hall. You will see a table and sign.

Volunteers are welcome to help Jason man the table! If you have youth, please choose a time slot to bring your child to help out. As this money goes to the youth we want them to be a part of the process and hard work it takes to raise the money!

There is a sign up sheet below for volunteers. I've carved out two hour chunks for youth to help so the 8-10, 10-12, 12-2 time slots are for youth.

Depending on the number of items, we may need someone to help us haul things to the Big Glades the morning of August 8 as well, so I added a volunteer spot for that.
